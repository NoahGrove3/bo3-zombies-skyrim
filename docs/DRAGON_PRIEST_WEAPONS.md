# Dragon Priest weapons

Noah's design (Nov 2026). Each of the ten Dragon Priests drops its own weapon the first time it is killed.

- **Who:** any class can wield them.
- **Power:** stronger than Echo Altar weapons, below the class wonders.
- **Abilities:** every hit carries the priest's theme (a passive), and the heavy attack is a signature power.
- **Kept:** for the rest of the run, like wonders.
- **Look:** "Our own models with 2k textures and hyper realistic detail and effects. I want each weapon to feel and play as good as they look."
  Modelled from scratch in `tools/` (as `mutagenstaff.py`, `wyrmhorn.py`), a render of each approved before it goes in.
- Miraak does not drop one: the ten only.
