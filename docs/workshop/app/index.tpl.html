Abilities

What each weapon, wonder and flask does when it strikes, and its own effect, rebuilt from real parts: the real particle effects of Skyrim, Dawnguard, Dragonborn, Apocalypse and Mysticism (fire, smoke, sparks, shockwaves) put together into one new effect, in its own colour and size, each part on its own beat. Each card lists the parts. Click a weapon (or one of its abilities) to open it: five options of its own - variations of what that ability actually does - any colour, and sliders for size, brightness, area of effect (which also sets how far it reaches the dead in game), vibrance, speed, linger, density and light cast. Every change shows here at once and saves; the game takes your picks next build. The class spells play their own effects, from their own mods.

Sounds

Every weapon's own sounds - drawn, swung, swung heavy, and its hit on the dead - and every ability's: its cast, its impact, and what lingers after. Each has five options: the first is what the game plays now, the one marked my pick is my recommendation. All of them are real sounds from your own install (Skyrim, Dawnguard, Dragonborn, Apocalypse, Mysticism) or ours. Press ▶ to hear any of them; Hear it play runs the weapon in 3D with every sound on its beat. Every sound plays at 20% - background, as you asked. Tune each with volume, pitch, variation (so a hundred hits don't sound the same) and a second sound layered under it. Your picks save here; the game takes them when you call the doc final.
