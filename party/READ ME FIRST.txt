UNDYING NIGHTS - PARTY PACK
===========================

What you need: Skyrim Special Edition (or Anniversary Edition) on Steam, up to date, run once.

JOINING (friends)
 1. If Noah uses Tailscale: install Tailscale (tailscale.com), sign in, and accept Noah's invite.
 2. Close Skyrim. Double-click  JOIN THE PARTY.bat  (Windows may ask "Run anyway" - it's the installer).
 3. Open "Undying Nights Co-op" on your desktop. Load a save or make a character.
 4. Open the Skyrim Together menu in game, choose Connect, paste the address (already copied for you)
    and type the password from Party.txt.
 5. You're in Noah's party. When night falls, his game raises the dead and you fight them together.

HOSTING (Noah)
 1. Fill in Party.txt (your Tailscale address and a password) before sharing this folder.
 2. Double-click  HOST THE PARTY (Noah only).bat  once.
 3. Each session: open "Undying Nights Server", then "Undying Nights Co-op", connect to 127.0.0.1.

GOOD TO KNOW
 - Nights are run by the host: only Noah's game raises the horde, events and bosses; you all see and fight them.
 - Your points, weapons, brews, Reforge levels and city quests are saved in YOUR OWN save.
 - Going down: Skyrim Together's death system picks you back up (no save loss).
 - Graphics: Noah's look comes from optional mods (Skyland, Azurite Weathers, Realistic Water Two, Lux, Lux Via,
   Happy Little Trees, Skoglendi, SMIM). They are not needed to play together.
 - Uninstall: delete BO3Zombies.asi, dinput8.dll and UndyingNights.ini from the Skyrim folder and
   Data\BO3Zombies.esp, Data\SkyrimTogetherReborn.
