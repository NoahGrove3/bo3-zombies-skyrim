THE CURSE OF ALDUIN - PARTY PACK
================================

You need: Skyrim Special Edition or Anniversary Edition on Steam, updated, started once. Both players need
this SAME pack (an older pack loads an older build of the mod, or none at all). This pack replaces the
old "Undying Nights" pack - the mod was renamed; old Undying Nights saves will not load, start a new character.

ONE-TIME SETUP (both players)
 1. Close Skyrim.
 2. Unzip this pack anywhere (Desktop is fine) and double-click  JOIN THE PARTY.bat
    (the host - Noah - runs  HOST THE PARTY (Noah only).bat  instead).
    If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": More info > Run anyway.
 3. It finds Skyrim, installs The Curse of Alduin + Skyrim Together Reborn + the party mods (below), turns the
    plugins on and puts a "The Curse of Alduin Co-op" shortcut on your desktop.

EVERY TIME YOU PLAY
 1. Start the game with the "The Curse of Alduin Co-op" shortcut on your desktop. NOT the Steam Play button, not the
    Skyrim launcher - those start Skyrim without Skyrim Together, so you can't see each other.
 2. Wait for the Curse of Alduin main menu (the Echo Altar over a lake, with CO-OP PARTY in its list). If you see the normal Skyrim menu,
    the mod didn't load - run the .bat again and check the Troubleshooting list below.
 3. HOST (Noah): in the main menu choose  CO-OP PARTY  (in the list on the right), then  HOST A PARTY.
    The Steam overlay opens - invite your friend.
 4. FRIEND: stay in the Curse of Alduin main menu and accept the invite there - press Shift+Tab to open the
    Steam overlay and click Accept - or choose  CO-OP PARTY  then  JOIN <Noah's name>.
    Do not accept the invite from Steam while the game is closed: Steam would start plain Skyrim.
 5. Both: load a save or start a new character. Skyrim Together connects you by itself (no address, no
    password to type). Each of you starts where your own save is - meet up in game (Skyrim Together's
    party list shows the others).

GOOD TO KNOW
 - A new character starts as a Spellsword: a steel sword in the right hand, Firebolt in the left. The
   shrine's Coffer teaches more spells (they land in the Magic menu); Reforge makes the one in hand stronger.
 - Settings: Esc > System > Mod Configuration > The Curse of Alduin (field of view, townsfolk indoors at night,
   difficulty, the shrine's keys) and Other mods. Skyrim's own difficulty slider now sets the night's dead too.
 - Survival Mode is switched off in The Curse of Alduin games (hunger and cold fight the nights). - The host's game runs the nights: the horde, events and bosses. You both see and fight them.
 - Points, spells, brews, Reforge levels and your city quests are kept in YOUR OWN save.
 - Going down: at zero health you fall for 30 seconds. A friend who stands by you and holds E for 3 seconds
   picks you up. If nobody does, you die and watch the others until a rite is finished or dawn comes - then
   you rise. No fall damage: falls never hurt.
 - SAVING: every game saves itself at each dawn ("GAME SAVED - Dawn after Night N"), all at the same moment,
   and when a city is freed (the save is named for it). If the whole party dies at once, everyone's game
   loads that same dawn save - so you all come back together. You can still save yourself any time.
 - Noah's look comes from graphics mods, and they are all in this pack (files\Graphics, about 25 GB):
   the installer asks if you want them. Say Y to see the game exactly as the host does, N to keep your own.
   They are: Community Shaders (+ Subsurface Scattering, Screen Space Shadows, Grass Collision), Azurite Weathers III, Lux Orbis + Lux Via (lighting, roads), Realistic Water Two, Happy Little
   Trees, Skyland AIO, SMIM, Lit Road Signs, Cathedral Armory, Lux,
   Riton's Medieval Solitude, Stony AF Markarth, Venerable Nordic Temples, SRP 2K (landscape, dungeons,
   architecture, misc), Skysight Skins, the Icy pack (Icy Fixes, Mesh, Cave and Biome Remaster), and the grass:
   Tamrielic Grass, Veydosebrom Regions, Folkvangr, Seasonal Landscapes, Short Grass Mixed, Cathedral 3D Pine Grass,
   Origins of Forest (with their complex-grass textures, lit by Community Shaders). For frame rate: Community
   Shaders' Frame Generation (turn it on in the Community Shaders menu, Home key) and Skyrim Priority.

TROUBLESHOOTING
 - Normal Skyrim menu / you start on the Helgen cart: The Curse of Alduin isn't loaded. Close Skyrim, run the
   .bat again, start from the desktop shortcut. The installer prints your Skyrim version - it must be
   current (Steam: right-click Skyrim > Properties > Updates > keep it updated).
 - Antivirus removed dinput8.dll or CurseOfAlduin.asi from the Skyrim folder: allow them and run the .bat again.
 - Accepted the invite and nothing happened: you were not in the Curse of Alduin main menu, or one of you
   has an older pack. Both re-run the .bat from this pack, start from the shortcut, try again.
 - Nothing loads (plain Skyrim menu, no mods): your Skyrim must be the current Steam version (1.7.104) - the
   installer says which you have. SKSE (skse64_1_7_104.dll, skse64_loader.exe) must be in the Skyrim folder;
   run the .bat again (right-click > Run as administrator if Skyrim is under Program Files).
 - Uninstall: delete CurseOfAlduin.asi, dinput8.dll and CurseOfAlduin.ini from the Skyrim folder and
   Data\CurseOfAlduin.esp, Data\SkyrimTogetherReborn.
